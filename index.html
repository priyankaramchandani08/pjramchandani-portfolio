    Systems Intern, DPWH PPP ServiceJanuary 2026

    My experience as a Systems Intern at the DPWH Public-Private Partnership Service gave me an early look at how technology and digital systems can support real-world engineering and infrastructure projects. I observed how digital systems were used to monitor road conditions, infrastructure projects, and project progress. This experience helped me understand that engineering is not only about designing things but also about using technology and information effectively to solve practical problems. As an Artificial Intelligence Engineering student, it also made me more interested in how data and intelligent systems can be used to improve decision-making and monitoring.

    Scientific Research ProjectSynthesis of Oleophilic Briquettes from Rice Hull and Coconut Husk via Thermal Treatment Process

    Working on our research project taught me how important data, experimentation, and careful analysis are when solving an engineering problem. We tested the briquettes for oil absorption, water absorption, density, and calorific value, then analyzed our results using Excel and statistical methods. I learned that results do not always turn out the way you expect, and that understanding the data is just as important as conducting the experiment. This experience strengthened my interest in data analysis and gave me skills that I can carry into AI Engineering, where decisions also depend heavily on reliable data.
